### Top lessons
1. Coin-flip moneylines (-110 to -150) killed the long tickets: 0-4 in the morning Best Shot. Lottery legs need ~75%+ true probability.
2. Big NBA spreads (-12 or more) are fragile in garbage time: Celtics -15.5 won by 14 and missed twice.
3. Do not carry a failed-risk leg into multiple tickets; correlated reuse turned one miss into three losses (Kent St, Kraken, Celtics, Lakers).
4. Pace/goalie-driven overs and injury-usage unders/props were the best angles (Overs parlay won +599; singles 2nd run 5-1).
5. Props: check projected minutes/role, and only take lines ≥1.5 below recent average; rebounds lines at the average were coin flips.
6. Fewer legs on 'safe' parlays: 3 of 3 morning parlays lost by exactly one leg or two — trim to 2-3 strongest legs.
